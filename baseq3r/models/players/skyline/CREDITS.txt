Q3Rally Skyline - asset sources
===============================

body.md3, wheel.md3, lights.tga, tlite.tga, grill.tga
    Based on "Nissan Skyline R34 GT-R" by Lexyc16
    https://skfb.ly/6TxFX (Sketchfab)
    License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

    Changes for Q3Rally (tools/carbuild/r34):
    - converted from OBJ to MD3, scaled to the size of the other cars and
      centred on the origin; the full geometry of the body is kept
      (38,624 triangles), split into four paint surfaces to stay below the
      engine's per-surface limit
    - manufacturer emblem on the boot lid and the logo texture left out
    - new UVs for the paint (five-sided projection atlas) and the trim parts;
      lamps and grille keep their original UVs and textures (resized)
    - wheel: the original tyre at full detail with new UVs for the shared
      Q3Rally rim and tread textures, the rim opening closed with a dished
      disc; the modelled rims are not used
    - tags for wheels, lights, plate, exhaust, roof and bonnet

*_main.tga, glass.tga, chrome.tga, trim.tga, under.tga, icon_*.tga
    Made for Q3Rally (tools/carbuild/r34/gen_textures.py, render3d.py).

sound/player/skyline/horn.ogg, falling1.ogg
    Shared with the Mustang and Camaro.
