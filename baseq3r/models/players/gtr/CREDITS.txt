Q3Rally GT-R - asset sources
============================

body.md3, wheel.md3, parts.tga
    Based on NissanGT-R.fbx, a Nissan GT-R (R35) model provided by the
    Q3Rally project (own model, same license as the game).

    Changes for Q3Rally (tools/carbuild/gtr):
    - only the triangles visible from outside are kept (cull.py): the
      interior and the engine of the source are left out; 103,341 of
      757,016 triangles remain, decimated to 100,007 (mesh.py, pyfqmr)
      with the borders between parts kept in place
    - scaled to the size of the other cars and centred on the origin
    - manufacturer badges left out
    - the source has no textures: the paint gets a five-sided projection
      atlas, the other parts their material colours from a palette
      (parts.tga), headlamp and tail lamp colours brightened
    - wheel: the source tyre decimated to 3,000 triangles with UVs for the
      shared Q3Rally rim and tread textures, the rim opening closed with a
      dished disc; the modelled rims, brake discs and calipers are not used
    - tags for wheels, lights, plate, exhaust, roof and bonnet

*_main.tga, glass.tga, chrome.tga, icon_*.tga
    Made for Q3Rally (tools/carbuild/gtr/gen_textures.py, render3d.py).

sound/player/gtr/horn.ogg, falling1.ogg
    Shared with the Mustang, Camaro and Skyline.
