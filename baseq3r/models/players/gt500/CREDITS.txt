Q3Rally GT500 - asset sources
=============================

body.md3, wheel.md3, parts.tga
    Based on upload.fbx, a Shelby GT500 model provided by the Q3Rally
    project (own model, same license as the game).

    Changes for Q3Rally (tools/carbuild/gt500):
    - only the triangles visible from outside are kept (cull.py): the
      interior, the engine and most of the suspension of the source are
      left out
    - scaled to the size of the other cars and centred on the origin
    - manufacturer badges and lettering left out
    - panels wound inside out in the source turned the right way round
    - the source has no textures: the paint gets a five-sided projection
      atlas, the other parts their material colours from a palette
      (parts.tga), lamp colours brightened
    - wheel: a tyre turned on a lathe from the outline of the source tyre
      (1,792 triangles) with UVs for the shared Q3Rally rim and tread
      textures, the rim opening closed with a
      dished disc; the modelled rims and brakes are not used
    - tags for wheels, lights, plate, exhaust, roof and bonnet

*_main.tga, glass.tga, chrome.tga, icon_*.tga
    Made for Q3Rally (tools/carbuild/gt500/gen_textures.py, render3d.py).

sound/player/gt500/horn.ogg, falling1.ogg
    Shared with the GT-R, Mustang, Camaro and Skyline.
