Q3Rally default engine sound - asset sources
=============================================

on_00.wav .. on_10.wav
    Engine loops at constant rpm. Re-cut on 2026-10-01 from the former
    sound/player/sidepipe/engine0..10.wav (Q3Rally project assets) to whole
    repeating patterns with a 10 ms crossfade. Fundamentals are listed in
    engine.cfg.

pop1.wav .. pop4.wav
    Backfires. Synthetic placeholders generated on 2026-10-01 (filtered noise
    and a decaying sine, numpy). No third-party material.

shift1.wav, shift2.wav
    Gear change clack, cut from the Pixabay sound effect
    "car gear shift park chevrolet equinox" (Pixabay ID 267611,
    original file nsqke_sfx-car-gear-shift-park-chevrolet-equinox-267611.mp3).
    Edited for Q3Rally: two single clacks cut out (at 0.83 s and 3.50 s),
    110 Hz high-pass against the engine rumble, noise gate after the clack,
    normalized to -6 dBFS, 44.1 kHz mono.
