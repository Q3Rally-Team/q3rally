!!! --- Q3Rally Changelog --- !!!

unreleased

Added:

- New map q3r_canyonrush (Canyon Rush): A2B sprint through a red desert canyon,
  about 34,000 units from the start gate to the finish gate with six
  checkpoints: a gorge with a natural rock arch, a basin with hoodoos and a
  dry gravel riverbed, crests and banked bends. Generator
  tools/maptools/make_canyonrush.py; sandstone strata texture and ground
  shaders in textures/ and scripts/q3r_canyonrush.*.

- New map q3r_mesaking (Mesa King) for King of the Hill: round red rock
  arena with a mesa in the middle; the hill is its flat top, reached by four
  ramps on the diagonals. Mirror symmetric red/blue spawns and pickups.
  Generator tools/maptools/make_mesaking.py (uses the canyon ground set).

- Live score on the stadium scoreboard: new client side map entity
  rally_scoreboard (like rally_fx_spray, the server ignores it). Placed at
  the centre of the q3r_scoreboard model it draws red score, blue score and
  the match clock on every screen; goals make the scorer's number blink and
  show GOAL!, the kick-off shows KICK-OFF. Keys angle, sides, dist, width,
  height, range (entities.def). Added to q3r_sundown.

- Water with real-time reflections (OpenGL2 renderer): shaders with the new
  q3r_water keyword are drawn by a GLSL program that mirrors the scene in the
  water plane, ripples the reflection with two scrolling normal maps, mixes it
  with the water colour by a Fresnel term (see-through from above, mirror at
  grazing angles) and adds a sun glint from the map's q3map_sun. No
  misc_portal_surface is needed for it. Keywords: q3r_water <normalmap> [tile]
  [speed] [distortion], q3r_waterColor, q3r_waterFresnel, q3r_waterSpecular;
  r_water 0 switches it off. OpenGL1 draws the shader's normal stages instead.
  Example: textures/q3r_liquids/q3r_water_mirror (normal map water_normal.tga)
  in the test map q3r_watertest. The pool in q3r_valley (dip_valley/water)
  uses it too; on water surfaces deformVertexes only applies to the OpenGL1
  stages.

- Real-time sun shadows (OpenGL2): cars, props and items cast shadows from
  the map's sun that move with them. Only models are rendered into the camera
  shadow cascades; the map's own shadows stay baked, and a car shadow is only
  drawn where the map is in the sun (no second shadow in the shade, shaded
  areas are no longer darkened twice, and the map is not redrawn for the
  shadows every frame). New cvar r_sunShadowCasters (1 = models only, 0 =
  ioquake3 behaviour). The blob under the cars stays as a lighter contact
  shadow. Switched on through q3gl2_sun in the sky shaders of q3r_valley,
  q3r_country01, q3r_lcs1, q3r_mk, q3r_dirtyfun, q3r_ridgeracer, q3r_lavafalls
  and q3r_sk1. The Ultra graphics preset uses sunlight mode 1 instead of 2
  (mode 2 adds the sun on top of the lightmap); the modes are now called
  Off / Shadows / Deluxe.

- Lamp shadows (OpenGL2): cars, props and items also cast real-time shadows
  from the map's lamps. Each frame the renderer picks the lamps that light the
  models near the camera most (r_lampShadows = how many, 0..4, default 2) and
  renders a small depth map from each lamp: point lights look straight down,
  spotlights along their cone. The map part of a lamp's depth map is rendered
  once when the lamp is picked, only the models every frame. Like the sun
  shadows, the shadow is only laid onto the map, not onto other cars, and only
  where the lamp's light actually reaches (so no shadow behind a wall); it
  gets weaker with the distance to the lamp and fades in when a lamp is
  picked. Cvars r_lampShadows, r_lampShadowStrength (0.7) and
  r_lampShadowMapSize (512, latched). q3map2 normally removes the light
  entities from the BSP; maps must keep them (q3map2 -bsp -keeplights, or
  tools/maptools/inject_lights.py for compiled maps). The lights were added
  to q3r_nightcity, q3r_demobowl, q3r_babel, q3r_ctf_04, q3r_david5,
  q3r_lavafalls, q3r_dd_02, q3r_ctf_01, q3r_david4 and q3r_dm02 (AAS
  checksums updated). The rear view and the minimap do not draw lamp shadows.

- Headlights (OpenGL2): each car with its lights on now has one real spot
  light instead of a row of round additive glows. The road, walls and other
  cars are lit in a cone with their own colours (an extra pass per surface
  that multiplies the light with the surface texture), and the headlights
  nearest to the camera cast shadows of the map and the cars (a shadow map
  per light, rendered every frame; the car the light belongs to is behind it
  and not included). New cvars r_headlights (0 = the old look),
  r_headlightIntensity (2.5), r_headlightShadows (how many lights cast
  shadows, 0-2, default 2) and r_headlightShadowMapSize (1024, latched).
  New cgame import trap_R_AddSpotLightToScene (CG_R_ADDSPOTLIGHTTOSCENE = 98);
  OpenGL1 draws such a light as three point lights along the beam, as
  before. Engine, both renderers and cgame must be updated together.

- Tail, brake and reverse lights (OpenGL2) use the same spot lights: one
  wide cone per car from behind the rear light tags onto the road, walls and
  the car behind, with their colours (red light on green grass stays dark,
  as it should). The tail light glows weakly while the headlights are on,
  the brake light replaces it when braking, the reverse light is white.
  They cast no shadows, the shadow maps stay with the headlights (new spot
  light flag SPOTLIGHT_NOSHADOW). OpenGL1 and r_headlights 0 draw one
  additive point light behind the car instead of one per lamp.

- Car paint reflections (OpenGL2): car paint and glass now reflect the real
  surroundings instead of a fixed picture. A cube map around the camera is
  rendered from the map, the sky and the cars further away (one side per
  frame, the whole cube every six frames) and the $dynamicreflection stages
  of the car shaders look it up along the reflected view direction, with a
  Fresnel term (stronger towards the edges). Car paint also gets a clear
  coat: a sharp reflection on top of the paint, weak seen straight on and
  strong at grazing angles. New cvars r_carReflections (0 = the old fixed
  reflection, 1 = one side per frame, 2 = all sides every frame),
  r_carReflectionSize (256, latched) and r_carReflectionStrength (1).
  OpenGL1 keeps the fixed reflection.

- Motion blur (OpenGL2): the surroundings blur with the camera motion at
  speed and in fast turns, the road right next to the car included; the
  car in front of the camera stays sharp (its screen rectangle and depth
  are left out). Every pixel is traced back to where it was on the screen
  in the previous frame (depth and last frame's camera), so the blur
  follows the real motion and looks the same at any frame rate. It gets
  much stronger while the viewed car burns NOS (new refdef flag RDF_NOS
  from the cgame, blended in and out over 0.3 s). New cvars r_motionBlur
  (0.5, share of a 1/60 s frame, 0-2, 0 = off), r_motionBlurNos (1.5, 0-4),
  r_motionBlurMax / r_motionBlurMaxNos (longest blur, 0.05 / 0.12 of the
  screen) and r_motionBlurNear (120, fade-in distance from the camera).

- Tyre smoke and dust: skidding tyres and tyres rolling over loose ground
  throw up soft, billowing puffs (new texture gfx/misc/q3r_smoke.tga) that
  grow, turn, slow down, rise (smoke) or settle (dust), carry along a part
  of the car's speed and are tinted with the light of the map where they
  start, so smoke no longer glows in the dark. Own colours and behaviour
  for asphalt (white smoke), dust, dirt, gravel, sand and grass; sand now
  dusts at speed as well. They run in the client effect particle pool
  (cg_rally_fx.c, now 1024 particles); new cvar cg_tireSmoke (1, 0 = off,
  up to 2 = denser). Snow, wet road and ice keep their sprays.
- Soft particles (OpenGL2): smoke, dust, spray and other sprites and
  particle polys fade out where they cut into the ground and walls instead
  of showing a hard edge (they read the depth copy the renderer already
  makes after the depth prepass, tr.hdrDepthImage; a missing depth leaves
  them as they are). New cvar r_softParticles (24 = fade distance in
  units, 0 = off; needs r_depthPrepass 1).
- Rear weapons: the smoke screen is a thick, lit cloud that hangs on the
  track for several seconds (it was a few faint puffs), and a mine
  explosion throws up dark smoke and dirt. All rear weapons are now
  registered at map load: a weapon handed out without a pickup in the map
  (give, game modes) showed no mine model and a missing-texture square
  instead of the explosion.
- Ladder and weather: every match report says what the match was driven
  in (weather.condition dry, wet, snow or mixed, from the share of the
  time the ground out in the weather was wet or snowed in, without warmup
  and intermission; also the share in percent, g_weather and what the map
  allows). The ladder page (1.0.17) has a weather filter next to Online /
  Offline: any weather, dry, wet, snow; matches from before count as dry.
  Laps on a wet or snowy track are no longer uploaded as ghosts. The match
  payload got new fields at its end: game module and engine must be
  updated together.
- Maps set up for the weather (worldspawn keys, without compiling them
  again; the .map files have the same keys): rain on q3r_country01 (50 %),
  q3r_sk1 (70 %), q3r_ctf_01 (60 %), q3r_nightcity (50 %), q3r_downtown,
  q3r_citydom, q3r_babel (40 %), q3r_dd_02, q3r_demobowl, q3r_dirtyfun
  (30 %) and q3r_valley (15 %); snow on q3r_ridgeracer (60 %, wind 60 to
  300); no weather on q3r_lavafalls, q3r_battlecanyon, q3r_david4,
  q3r_dm02, q3r_ctf_04, q3r_lcs1 and q3r_mk. The sky of q3r_babel,
  q3r_dirtyfun, q3r_ridgeracer, q3r_lcs1 and q3r_mk is now marked as sky
  in the BSP (it was compiled without the flag, so no weather could fall
  there); the old weather zones of q3r_ridgeracer and q3r_sk1 are gone.
- Tyre sounds in snow and on wet ground: tyres no longer squeal on snow
  or ice (lying snow from the weather or snow surfaces of the map); they
  crunch through the snow while they roll, louder with the speed and when
  the car slides, and hiss on wet ground. The squeal of a skid on hard
  ground stays. New sound sound/rally/car/snow_roll.wav; the wet hiss is
  sound/weather/wet_tire_spray_loop.wav.
- Overcast (OpenGL2): while it rains or snows the sky clouds over, in
  slowly drifting grey patches, and the light follows the intensity of the
  shower: the sun gets weak and its shadows fade, the patches of sunlight
  baked into the map dim, the far distance disappears in a grey haze
  (thicker in snow, also on maps without a haze of their own, no glow
  towards the sun), and the picture gets a little darker and its colours
  duller (less in snow). When the shower passes the sky clears with it.
- Wind: the weather now has a wind that every client sees the same. It
  turns and changes with every change of the weather over the same 30 to
  60 seconds, is stronger with rain or snow and has gusts on top. It
  slants the rain, blows the snow along (instead of the random gusts each
  client made up) and carries tyre smoke, dust, wet and snow spray,
  smoke puffs and waterfall mist downwind: fully out in the open, a
  little under a roof. Out in the open a wind sound comes from the side
  the wind blows from, louder the stronger it is (s_weatherVolume,
  cg_weatherAudio). New worldspawn keys weatherWind ("30 250", min and
  max speed in units per second, "0 0" = calm) and weatherWindDir
  (prevailing direction, -1 = any). weatherinfo prints the wind. The
  wind does not push the cars.
- Snow on the ground (OpenGL2): snow from the weather settles where the
  sky is above, on the flat ground (it slides off steep slopes and walls).
  It starts as a thin powder, then the big drifts close up until the
  ground is white; grass and loose ground get covered first, asphalt last.
  When it melts the patches open again and leave the ground wet. The snow
  is lit by the lightmap of the surface (or its vertex light) and the sun
  and lamp shadows, with wind ripples and a few glittering crystals close
  by. With the automatic exposure the camera opens up by up to 0.4 stops
  on snow, so it does not turn grey. Surfaces that are already
  snow (SURF_SNOW, SURF_ICE) are left as they are. On lying snow the rear
  tyres leave ruts while rolling, not only when they skid (longer ribbon
  segments, so they do not use up the marks); marks in snow are lighter
  than on other ground (skids 60 %, rolling ruts 40 %). New cheat cvar
  cg_weatherPreview "wet snow" (e.g. "0.5 0.5") shows the ground at that
  wetness and snow, for mappers and screenshots; the grip does not change.
- Wet ground (OpenGL2): inside a rain zone (rally_weather_rain) the ground
  gets wet within about 20 seconds while it rains there and dries within
  about 2 minutes after the rain stops; outside the rain zones it stays
  dry (each zone has its own wetness, soft at its sides). Wet
  ground is darker and reflects the scene (screen space reflections:
  the reflected view ray is stepped through the depth of the frame and the
  colour is taken from the previous frame), with Fresnel (strong at
  grazing angles) and puddles on asphalt that mirror the surroundings,
  lights and cars. Asphalt, concrete and metal reflect most, dirt, sand
  and gravel mostly get darker, grass only a little; walls get a bit
  darker and do not mirror. New worldspawn key "wetness" (0 to 1) for maps
  that are wet without rain, e.g. a night city after the rain; shaders
  with surfaceparm wet are always wet. New cvars r_wetGround (1, 0 = off,
  2 = finer reflections; needs r_depthPrepass 1) and r_wetness (cheat,
  test value, -1 = from the rain). OpenGL1 draws the ground as before.
  Puddles form on every kind of ground once it is properly wet and grow
  with the wetness; they mirror like water (muddy on dirt, sand and
  grass), and while it rains drops make rings in them.
- Tyre spray on wet roads and powder snow behind the tyres are soft, lit
  clouds in the client particle pool like the tyre smoke (they followed the
  map's light too little and were small sprites); wet ground no longer
  throws up dust.
- q3r_sk1 (Canyon Cruise): the puddle on the track and the creek under the
  bridges use the reflective water (new shader
  textures/q3r_liquids/q3r_water_pool, the old pool look stays for
  OpenGL1 and r_water 0; liquids/calm_poollight itself is unchanged for
  q3r_ctf_04 and q3r_david4).
- Skid marks are continuous tracks: each tyre lays one ribbon that follows
  the line it drives (also in tight circles), with a tread pattern along
  the track, instead of a row of overlapping square stamps with gaps and
  dark overlaps. Own textures for asphalt, dirt, grass, sand, snow and ice
  (the snow, sand and ice marks were drawn as black bands); short breaks
  of the wheel slip no longer cut the track. Marks stay 20 seconds and
  fade out over the last 4 seconds; more marks at once (8192 polys).
- Filmic tone mapping and colour grading (OpenGL2, r_hdr 1 and r_toneMap
  1): a new tone curve (ACES, in linear light) gives deeper shadows, more
  depth in the mid tones and bright parts that roll off softly instead of
  clipping (sunlit concrete, lamps, yellow city walls keep their detail).
  After it every pixel goes through a colour grading LUT, the look of the
  map. Looks in gfx/luts: q3r_default (subtle, used when the map sets
  none), warm, cold, night, vivid, overcast, dusk and neutral. A map picks
  its look with the worldspawn key "colorlut" or with
  "q3r_colorLut <image>" in its sky shader (no recompile needed). New
  cvars r_toneMapCurve (1 = filmic, 0 = the old curve), r_filmicExposure
  (1.25), r_filmicWhite (2), r_colorGrade (strength, 1, 0 = off) and
  r_colorLut (one look for every map, "none" = off). New looks are made
  with tools/maptools/make_color_luts.py. Looks set in the sky shaders:
  warm for q3r_sk1, q3r_lcs1, q3r_mk, q3r_valley and q3r_lavafalls; night
  for q3r_nightcity, q3r_babel, q3r_demobowl, q3r_ctf_04, q3r_dm02,
  q3r_battlecanyon and q3r_david4; dusk for q3r_dirtyfun and q3r_dd_02;
  cold for q3r_ridgeracer (and q3r_watertest). The other maps use
  q3r_default.
- Ambient occlusion (OpenGL2): soft shadows where surfaces meet, so the
  cars stand on the ground (a contact shadow under the car) and kerbs,
  wall bases, corners and props get depth. Rebuilt after Scalable Ambient
  Obscurance: positions and normals from the depth of the prepass, 12
  samples (24 with r_ssao 2) on a spiral with a radius in world units, at
  half resolution with the depth aware blur; it fades out in the distance.
  Only the main view. r_ssao is now on by default (latched, needs
  r_depthPrepass 1); new cvars r_ssaoRadius (48), r_ssaoStrength (0.8) and
  r_ssaoDistance (3000).
- Normal and specular maps for the map textures (OpenGL2): textures without
  their own _n / _s maps get them made when the map loads, from the
  texture itself (relief from its brightness with painted light and shadow
  taken out, at a fine and a coarse scale; gloss lower in the gaps).
  Relief, reflectance and gloss follow the material from the surface flags:
  asphalt slightly glossy, dirt, sand and grass matt, gravel rough, metal
  shiny, ice glossy. Only world surfaces with a lightmap (no models, car
  skins, decals or alpha tested surfaces). The old ioquake3 generator
  (one pixel Sobel, brightened the texture) is no longer used.
  r_genNormalMaps is now on by default (latched); new cvar
  r_genNormalStrength (1). Real _n / _s maps next to a texture still win.
- Distance and ground haze (OpenGL2): the air between the camera and far
  surfaces is hazy in the colour of the map's sky, so the distance gets
  depth; a ground haze can lie in valleys and hollows (thick near the
  ground, thin higher up, integrated along the view ray) and the haze
  glows towards the sun. The sky only gets haze at the horizon. Set in the
  sky shader with q3r_haze <r> <g> <b> <distance> [<ground distance>
  <ground height> <ground thickness> [<sun glow>]] or with the worldspawn
  key "haze" (same values, wins over the sky shader). Set for every map
  with a sky except the space nebula; country01 and sk1 also get ground
  haze. New cvars r_haze (1) and r_hazeDensity (1).
- OpenGL2: alpha tested surfaces that write depth (trees, fences, grates)
  are now part of the depth prepass even when they blend, so haze, ambient
  occlusion and soft particles see them (the tree tops above the forest
  walls were treated as sky).

- Weather system reworked. The weather now covers the whole map and falls
  wherever the sky is above: when a map loads, the server and every client
  trace it from above on a 64 unit grid and keep, per cell, the highest
  surface that sees a sky face; under bridges, roofs and in tunnels the
  ground stays dry (the rain blows a little way in under the edges). The
  server runs showers of rain or snow that build up over 30 to 60 seconds,
  last a while and pass, also during a race, with an intensity from 0 to
  1 instead of on/off. Every change goes to the clients as one config
  string (CS_WEATHER); server and clients work out the same wet and
  snowed in ground from it in fixed steps, so the grip on the server, the
  prediction and the look agree, also for late joiners. Wet ground loses
  grip in proportion to how wet it is (up to a quarter), fresh snow moves
  the grip towards packed snow; tyre spray and powder snow follow the
  ground. Rain wets the ground in about 20 seconds of heavy rain and it
  dries in about 2 minutes; snow covers it in about 90 seconds and melts
  in about 4 minutes, leaving it wet. A race that starts in the rain
  starts on a wet track. New worldspawn keys weather (none, rain, snow or
  "rain snow"), weatherChance (40), weatherCycle ("90 300" seconds) and
  weatherStrength ("0.4 1"). New server cvar g_weather (0 always dry, 1
  as the map says, 2 rain, 3 snow for the whole race on any map, e.g. for
  the ladder); Ghost Race is always dry. The cvars g_dynamicWeather,
  g_weatherInitialChance, g_weatherOnMinTime, g_weatherOnMaxTime,
  g_weatherOffMinTime and g_weatherOffMaxTime are gone. rally_weather_rain
  and rally_weather_snow are obsolete: a map that still has them and no
  weather key gets rain or snow. New client command weatherinfo and cheat
  r_weatherDebug 1 (OpenGL2: the ground blue where the weather reaches it,
  red where it is covered). New cgame import trap_R_SetWeatherGrid
  (CG_R_SETWEATHERGRID = 99); refdef_t carries weather[4] instead of the
  rain zones.

- Graphics menu reorganised into three pages: Graphics (preset, renderer,
  resolution, aspect ratio, fullscreen, anti-aliasing, texture detail,
  texture filter, geometry), Effects (bloom, motion blur, reflections,
  water, wet & snow, haze, colour look, ambient occlusion, surface relief,
  tyre smoke, soft particles, skid marks, tone curve) and Lighting &
  shadows (HDR, sun shadows and their quality, lamp shadows, headlights,

- Slipstream (drafting) in the race modes (not in Ghost Race). A car close behind
  another one loses part of its air drag and gets faster on the straights; since the
  down force follows the drag, it also has less grip in corners ("dirty air"). The
  wake is about 42 m long, widens with the distance, needs both cars moving fast in
  the same direction and is blocked by world geometry. The server measures the wake
  and sends a target in the new STAT_SLIPSTREAM; pmove ramps the value (0.5 s in,
  0.25 s out), so client prediction stays exact. Server cvars: g_slipstream (1),
  g_slipstreamStrength (0.25 = up to 25 % less drag), g_slipstreamDistance (1500 qu),
  all three part of the ladder rules. Laps driven in another car's slipstream are not
  uploaded as ladder ghosts. HUD gauge above the telemetry strip, cg_hudShowSlipstream

new in v0.8

Fixed:

- Race distance counter now works correctly on maps with single-brush checkpoint entities
  (e.g. q3r_valley). The counter was always zero on these maps because brush entities
  without an explicit "origin" key have s.origin == (0,0,0); the calculation now uses
  the center of r.absmin/r.absmax instead. Additionally, the start/finish entity was not
  being stored in the checkpoint array due to a late number assignment, leaving a NULL
  slot that froze the counter. The final segment from the last checkpoint to the finish
  line now counts down correctly, and the counter is clamped to zero once the player
  has finished the race.


new in v0.7c

Fixed:

- Dedicated server in DM mode was cycling through map rotation immediately without
  waiting for players. Root cause: GT_SPRINT was inserted into the gametype enum
  (bg_public.h) without explicit integer values, shifting GT_DEATHMATCH from 6 to 7
  and all team gametypes by one. A server configured with g_gametype 6 (DM) was
  therefore running GT_ELIMINATION (a race mode), which exits immediately when no
  players are present. Fixed by assigning explicit integer values to all gametype
  enum entries. Non-team gametypes retain their v0.6c values (0-6), GT_SPRINT is
  assigned 7. Team gametypes start at 16 to leave room for future non-team modes.
  Server configs for team-based servers (Team DM, CTF, CTF4, etc.) must be updated
  to the new values - see docs/gametype_values.md for the full mapping table.
