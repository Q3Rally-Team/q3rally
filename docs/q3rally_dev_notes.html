Hidden Graphics Features

CVars present in the renderer code but not exposed in any menu (neither the classic graphics menu nor the Advanced Graphics menu). Audit compares ui_video.c, ui_advanced_graphics.c against renderergl1/tr_init.c and renderergl2/tr_init.c.

Already in MenuGraphics: Preset, Renderer, Resolution, Aspect ratio, Fullscreen, Anti-aliasing (FXAA + MSAA), Texture detail, Texture filter (incl. aniso), Geometry. Effects: Bloom, Motion blur, Reflections, Water, Wet & snow, Haze, Colour look, Ambient occlusion, Surface relief, Tyre smoke, Soft particles, Skid marks, Tone curve. Lighting & shadows: HDR (with tone map and auto exposure), Sun shadows (with sunlight mode), Sun shadow quality, Lamp shadows, Headlights, Headlight shadows, Shadow detail, Dynamic lights. Taken out of the menu (cvars only): colour depth, extensions, texture bits, vertex light, sun rays, cascades, pshadows, Voodoo. (Dynamic Reflections was removed in v0.7d: the old r_dynamicReflections path rendered six nested scenes per frame, dropped the main view's entities and never reached the $dynamicreflection stages.)

Reflective Water (q3r_water) — Implementation

GL2-only water with a rippled planar reflection. Mapper usage is in the Manual (Mapping Reference → Reflective Water). Cvar r_water (archive, latched, default 1).

PartWhereWhat it does
Shader keywordsrenderergl2/tr_shader.c (ParseWaterParm, FinishWater)Reads q3r_water*. The water is only switched on when the reflection FBO, the water GLSL program and the normal map exist; then shader.isWater is set, vertex deforms are dropped (flat mirror plane) and the sort becomes SS_UNDERWATER (after the opaque world, so the floor shows through). Otherwise the shader's stages are used. A water shader may have no stages at all. GL1 skips the keywords (renderergl1/tr_shader.c).
Mirror viewrenderergl2/tr_main.c (R_SortDrawSurfs, R_GetWaterOrientations)If no classic portal was drawn, the first visible isWater surface renders a mirror view of its own plane (no misc_portal_surface needed), flagged VPF_WATERREFLECTION. PVS origin = viewer projected onto the plane, 8 units above it. One reflection per view.
Reflection texturetr_image.c, tr_fbo.c, tr_backend.ctr.waterReflectionImage/Fbo (screen size, HDR format). At the end of the mirror view RB_DrawSurfs blits the render target into it and sets backEnd.waterReflectionReady (reset in RB_SwapBuffers). Needs framebufferBlit; works with and without r_hdr/MSAA.
Water passtr_shade.c (RB_WaterPass), glsl/water_vp.glsl, glsl/water_fp.glslReplaces RB_IterateStagesGeneric for water surfaces; skipped inside portal views. Normal map projected along the surface's main axis, two scrolling layers; reflection looked up in screen space (gl_FragCoord * u_InvTexRes) with an offset that fades with distance; Fresnel mix with the water colour, alpha from Fresnel; sun glint from tr.sunLight/tr.sunDirection. Reuses existing uniforms (u_NormalScale, u_SpecularScale, u_Color, u_PrimaryLight*).
Open ideasRefraction of the floor (copy of the scene before the water pass), one reflection per water plane instead of per view, a time-sliced cubemap for car paint (as in noire-ioq3) to replace the removed dynamic reflections, and an Advanced Graphics menu entry for r_water.

Real-Time Sun Shadows — Implementation

Builds on the ioquake3 OpenGL2 cascaded sun shadow maps (r_sunlightMode 1, r_sunShadows 1, enabled per map by q3gl2_sun). Stock ioquake3 renders the whole map into every cascade and multiplies the result onto the lightmap, which darkens all baked shadow a second time and redraws the map four times per frame. Q3Rally changes that with r_sunShadowCasters 1 (default, archive, latched; 0 = stock). It only applies with r_sunlightMode 1; mode 2 adds the sun in the shader and needs the map in the shadow maps.

PartWhereWhat it does
Casterstr_main.c (R_RenderSunShadowMaps)Camera cascades 0–2 get only entity surfaces (cars, props, items). Cascade 3 (the whole level, cached until the sun direction changes, i.e. once per map) gets only the world. Its image is at least 2048² (tr_image.c); its world units per texel are stored in tr.sunShadowWorldTexel.
Shadow maskglsl/shadowmask_fp.glsl (USE_SHADOW_MODELS_ONLY), uniform u_ShadowParamsModel shadow from the first cascade that contains the pixel; world sunlight from cascade 3, sampled 3 texels towards the sun (no self-shadowing) and counted as sunlit when half of the PCF taps are lit. Result = 1 − (1 − model) × sun: a car shadow only where the map is in the sun.
Lightingglsl/lightall_fp.glsl (SHADOWMAP_MODELS_ONLY)The stock “surfaces facing away from the sun are shadowed” term is replaced by a fade of the mask on those surfaces, because the baked light already contains it. Shadowed light = light × q3gl2_sun strength.
Blobtr_bsp.c sets the ROM cvar r_sunShadowsActive after loading the world; cg_main.c reads it into cgs.sunShadows; cg_players.c (CG_PlayerShadow)With sun shadows the blob is drawn at half strength as a contact shadow.
Menuq3_ui/ui_advanced_graphics.cSunlight Mode items are Off / Shadows / Deluxe; the Ultra preset now uses Shadows (mode 2 adds the sun on top of the lightmap and is only for maps compiled with deluxemaps).
OpenGL1renderergl1/tr_shader.cSkips q3gl2_sun without a warning. Also maps $dynamicreflection to textures/reflect/reflect.jpg; before, every car shader using it failed and the cars were black in OpenGL1.
Open ideasFade the shadows out at the end of the last cascade instead of cutting them off, use a model LOD for the shadow pass, and headlight shadows at night (dlight cubemap shadows, r_dlightMode 2).

One spot light per car with its lights on, OpenGL2 only. Cvars: r_headlights (archive, 1; 0 = old point lights), r_headlightIntensity (archive, 2.5), r_headlightShadows (archive, 0..2, default 2), r_headlightShadowMapSize (archive, latched, 1024).

PartWhereWhat it does

Rear lightscg_players.c (CG_RearLight, TAILLIGHT_*, BRAKELIGHT_*, REVERSELIGHT_*)Glow models on tag_blite* / tag_rlite* as before; one spot from 8 units behind the middle of the tags, backwards and tilted 0.45 down, flag SPOTLIGHT_NOSHADOW (the two shadow maps stay with the headlights). Tail light (headlights on, not braking): radius 420, cone 150°, colour 0.15 0 0. Brake light (replaces it): 640, 140°, 0.6 0.02 0. Reverse light: 760, 110°, 0.4 0.4 0.37. Fallback (OpenGL1, r_headlights 0): a spot with a radius under 1000 becomes one additive point light 40 units along the beam, radius × 0.22, colour × 2.
Lighttr_local.h (dlight_t: spotDir, spotCosOuter/Inner, spotShadow, spotShadowMvp), tr_scene.c (RE_AddSpotLightToScene, refdef.spotMask), flags in renderercommon/tr_types.h (SPOTLIGHT_NOSHADOW → dlight_t.spotFlags)A spot is a dlight with a direction, so the world surface and bmodel culling of dlights is reused. Inner cone = 0.55 × outer.
Shadingtr_shade.c (ForwardSpotLights), glsl/spotlight_vp/fp.glslSpot bits are taken out of the normal dlight passes; one additive pass per spot (depth func equal) with the first texture stage that is not the lightmap as surface colour (tcGen texture, tcMods applied): colour × smoothstep cone × (1 − d²/r²)² × sqrt(N·L) (headlights hit the road at a grazing angle) × shadow. Normals use the lightall convention (visible side = GL back face). World surfaces get an identity model matrix (their transformMatrix is not set).
Shadowstr_main.c (R_RenderSpotShadowMaps), images *spotshadow0..1Main view only. The spots nearest to the camera (within radius + 1000, not SPOTLIGHT_NOSHADOW) get a perspective depth view every frame: world + RT_MODEL entities whose origin is at least 16 units in front of the light (the own car and its glow are behind it). PCF with 4 taps, sample moved towards the light and off the surface.
Modelstr_light.c (R_SetupEntityLighting)Spots light models only inside the cone and radius (no shadow); the old point light formula is not used for them.
NotesSurfaces with GPU vertex deforms get no headlight (the depth test of the extra pass does not match). Fog volumes do not fog the headlight pass. A light origin inside a wall (car pushed against it) shadows everything.

Car Paint Reflections — Implementation

OpenGL2 only. Cvars: r_carReflections (archive, 1; 0 = old fixed sphere map, 2 = all six sides every frame), r_carReflectionSize (archive, latched, 256), r_carReflectionStrength (archive, 1). The car shaders are unchanged: every stage with map $dynamicreflection (tcGen environment) is the reflection.

PartWhereWhat it does
Cube maptr_image.c (*carReflect, HDR format, mip mapped), tr_fbo.c (_carReflectFbo)Created whenever FBOs are available.
Renderingtr_main.c (R_RenderCarReflection), called from RE_RenderScene after the headlight shadow mapsMain view only, once per frame. One side per frame (tr.carReflectSide), centred on the camera, same side order and axes as R_RenderCubemapSide. View flag VPF_CARREFLECT: no mirrors/portals/water reflections, only RT_MODEL entities further than CAR_REFLECT_EXCLUDE (300) from the camera (the own car would cover a side). Dynamic lights, spot lights, lamp shadows and polys are switched off for these views; the entities are lit again for the main view afterwards. The backend attaches the side, clears it and builds the mip maps (tr_backend.c).
Shadingtr_shade.c (RB_CarReflectionStage, RB_DrawCarReflection), glsl/carreflect_vp/fp.glslThe $dynamicreflection stage keeps its blend (the paint layer above still sets how much shows) but samples the cube map at mip 1.5 along the reflected view direction × Schlick Fresnel from 0.5 seen straight on. Shaders whose reflection stage is opaque (paint) get a clear coat pass after all stages: additive, depth func equal, mip 0, Fresnel from 0.04, × 0.7. Constants CAR_REFLECT_* in tr_local.h. Not used inside the cube views themselves, for bone animated or deformed models.
NotesThe sides are stored like the ioq3 cube maps: direction d holds the view along −d, so the shader samples -R (lightall samples reflect(E, N) for the same reason). One cube for all cars, centred on the camera, so the reflections on cars far away are approximate. Cost: one extra 90° view of the map per frame at 256×256.

Motion Blur — Implementation

OpenGL2 post-process, main view only, before bloom (RB_MotionBlur in tr_postprocess.c, glsl/motionblur_vp/fp.glsl). Cvars (all archive): r_motionBlur (0.5, 0..2; 0 = off), r_motionBlurNos (1.5, 0..4), r_motionBlurMax (0.05), r_motionBlurMaxNos (0.12), r_motionBlurNear (120).

PartWhereWhat it does
ReprojectionRB_MotionBlurKeeps last frame's view-projection matrix (double precision). The shader gets last VP × inverse(this VP) and turns each pixel (screen position + depth from tr.renderDepthImage) into its screen position of the last frame; the difference is the blur direction. No blur on the first frame, after more than 100 ms, after a camera jump of more than 400 units or when the game time did not move.
StrengthRB_MotionBlurBlur = screen motion × strength × (16.7 ms / frame time), so the same at any frame rate. Strength and longest blur blend from r_motionBlur/r_motionBlurMax to r_motionBlurNos/r_motionBlurMaxNos over 300 ms while RDF_NOS is set.
Car maskR_ComputeMotionMask (tr_main.c, after the main R_RenderView), refdef.motionMaskRect/Far, motionblur_fp.glslThe car in front of the chase camera moves with it. The bounding boxes of all non-brush RT_MODEL entities within 400 units of the camera give a screen rectangle and a farthest view distance; pixels inside the rectangle that are not further away stay sharp, so the road right next to the car still blurs. Besides that the blur fades in from the camera up to r_motionBlurNear (linear depth from zNear/zFar). 12 samples along the motion with a per-pixel noise offset; samples on the car are left out, so it does not smear outwards.
NOS flagcg_view.c, renderercommon/tr_types.h (RDF_NOS = 0x40)Set while cg.snap->ps.powerups[PW_TURBO] is running (the viewed player, also when following). The NOS flame model on tag_turbo is no longer drawn; instead CG_AddRefEntityWithPowerups adds the light blue shell powerups/nos (all players see it).

Tyre Smoke, Dust & Soft Particles — Implementation

PartWhereWhat it does
Spawningcg_players.c (skid code, CG_TireSmokeKind)Skidding tyres: one puff every 60 ms per tyre, kind from the surface flags (dust, sand, grass, gravel, dirt, else asphalt smoke). Rolling over SURF_DUST / DIRT / GRAVEL / SAND: dust every 80–160 ms, stronger with speed. Snow, wet and ice keep their sprays and puffs.
Particlescg_rally_fx.c (CG_FX_TireSmoke, fxSmokeKinds, FXP_SMOKE)Same pool as rally_fx_spray (1024). Per kind: colour, alpha, start/end size, life, gravity (< 0 rises), drag, spread, upward speed and share of the car velocity. Grows with an ease-out, fades in over 6 % of its life and out quadratically, slows its spin. Colour × light grid 24 units above the start (clamped 0.3–1.1). No puffs beyond 4000 units; cg_tireSmoke thins (< 1) or thickens (> 1). Drawn as polys with gfx/misc/q3r_smoke (scripts/q3r_fx.shader).
Soft particlestr_backend.c (after the depth prepass), tr_surface.c, tr_shade.c, glsl/generic_fp.glslThe depth prepass already copies the opaque depth into tr.hdrDepthImage (R32F, now also created for r_softParticles); views that render into the main framebuffer set backEnd.softDepthReady. Sprites and polys set tess.softParticle; blended stages without depth write, polygon offset or decal sort get u_SoftParticle = (r_softParticles, blend mode, 1 / size) and the depth on texture unit 4 (TB_SPECULARMAP, never a depth-compare shadow map): fade = (scene depth − fragment depth) / distance, applied to alpha, colour (additive) or towards white (filter); a depth of 0 (no copy) leaves the particle unchanged.
Wet groundcg_view.c (CG_UpdateWetness), cg_rally_fx.c (worldspawn wetness), tr_shade.c (RB_WetGroundPass), tr_backend.c, glsl/wetground_*.glslrefdef_t.wetness is the map's own wetness everywhere; every rain zone (cg_atmospheric.c, CG_AtmosphericWetZones) has its own wetness that rises to 1 in 20 s while the zone is active (raining) and falls in 120 s when it is not. The wet zones go to the renderer in refdef_t.numWetZones / wetZones[MAX_WET_ZONES][8] (mins, wetness, maxs, raining; the 8 nearest to the view) and from there in u_WetZones (vec4 array, GLSL_SetUniformWetZones) to the shader, which takes the wettest zone per pixel (16 units soft at the sides, ground down to 64 units below the zone counts). r_wetness ≥ 0 overrides it for the whole map in RE_RenderScene. After its stages every opaque world surface of the main view gets an extra pass (depth equal, GL_ONE, GL_SRC_ALPHA): alpha darkens, colour adds a screen space reflection. The reflected ray is stepped (24, with r_wetGround 2 40 growing steps plus 5 bisections) through tr.hdrDepthImage; the hit is reprojected with tr.wetScenePrevViewProj into tr.wetSceneImage, last frame's HDR scene at half size with mip maps (copied in RB_PostProcess before tone mapping while the ground is wet; rough asphalt reads a blurred level). Rays that leave the screen or reach the sky take the colour there; material from the shader's surface flags (asphalt/default, dirt/dust/sand, gravel, grass; sky, snow, ice skipped), puddles from world space noise (they mirror like water on any ground, tinted by u_Color.rgb; zones where it rains add drop rings with u_Time). Tyre spray and snow dust are FX_SPRAY_WET / FX_SPRAY_SNOW puffs (CG_FX_Spray, thrown with a velocity, lit at least 0.65). Fixed: viewParms.zNear was never set (soft particles and motion blur got depth 0).
Skid markscg_marks.c (CG_SkidRibbon, CG_SkidRibbonStop), cg_players.c (CG_SurfaceEffects), gfx/skidmarks/*.tga, scripts/gfx.shaderEach tyre keeps a ribbon in centity_t (skidRibbonPoint/Side/Dist/Shader/Time). While it skids, a segment is laid every 14 units from the last point to the new one: a quad 14 units wide whose ends are turned across the direction of the old and the new segment (so the segments join without gaps or overlaps in curves), lifted 6 units and projected onto the ground with trap_CM_MarkFragments. R_MarkFragments needs the corners clockwise seen from above, the order is checked against the ground normal. s runs across the track, t along it (96 units per texture repeat, carried over from segment to segment). A new shader, a gap of more than 96 units or no slip for 150 ms starts a new ribbon. markPoly_t.lifeTime (20 s, fade over the last 4 s) overrides MARK_TOTAL_TIME. The textures darken (GL_ZERO GL_ONE_MINUS_SRC_COLOR, black = no mark); they are made by a script (tread blocks, soft edges, tinted per surface). MAX_POLYS 8192 / MAX_POLYVERTS 40000 in both renderers. On -meta maps the ground is SF_TRIANGLES, so marks need r_marksOnTriangleMeshes 1 (default now 1). The particles are added before the marks in CG_DrawActiveFrame so a full poly buffer drops marks, not smoke.
Rear weaponscg_rally_tools.c (CG_Hazard, HT_SMOKE), cg_weapons.c (CG_MissileHitWall, RWP_MINE), cg_main.cSmoke screen: 3 FX_SMOKE_SCREEN puffs per hazard event (the server sends about ten behind the car). Mine: 8 FX_SMOKE_BLAST + 4 dust puffs on top of the grenade explosion. Both ignore cg_tireSmoke. All rear weapons are registered in CG_RegisterGraphics, also when the map has no pickup for them.

Bloom & FXAA — Implementation

Post-processing passes of the OpenGL2 renderer, run in RB_PostProcess (tr_backend.c) for the main view only (viewport at least half the screen wide). Cvars: r_bloom (archive, latched, 1), r_bloomIntensity (archive, 0.4), r_bloomThreshold (archive, 0.85), r_fxaa (archive, 1). All need r_postProcess 1.

PartWhereWhat it does
Bloomtr_postprocess.c (RB_Bloom), glsl/bloom_fp.glsl, images *bloom0..3 (tr_image.c, tr_fbo.c)Runs after tone mapping on the LDR image. Bright pass + 4× downsample to 1/4 size (four bilinear taps, each weighted with smoothstep(threshold, 1, max(r,g,b))² so small lamps survive the averaging), 9-tap gaussian blur (5 bilinear taps) horizontally and vertically, downsample to 1/16 and blur again; both levels are added to the image (weights 0.8 and 0.4 × intensity). In Q3 content the lamp textures are not brighter than white, so a bright pass on the HDR image before tone mapping caught almost nothing; working on the clipped LDR image is what separates lamps from walls.
FXAARB_Fxaa, glsl/fxaa_fp.glslFXAA 3.11 (Timothy Lottes), PC quality path, 8 search steps per side, luma from the colour. Last pass before the 2D drawing: renderFbo → screenScratchFbo (now also created when r_fxaa is set) → back.
Open ideasSMAA instead of FXAA (sharper textures, needs the area/search lookup textures), a lens dirt texture for the bloom, per-map bloom settings in the worldspawn.

Ambient Occlusion — Implementation

Replaces the ioquake3 SSAO shader (3 samples, no normals). Cvars: r_ssao (archive, latched, 1; 2 = 24 samples), r_ssaoRadius (48), r_ssaoStrength (0.8), r_ssaoDistance (3000), all archive. Needs r_depthPrepass 1.

PartWhereWhat it does
AO passglsl/ssao_fp.glsl, RB_DrawSurfs (tr_backend.c, after the depth prepass)Scalable Ambient Obscurance (McGuire et al. 2012) at half resolution into tr.quarterFbo[0] (the "quarter" images are half size). The pixel is rebuilt in view space from tr.hdrDepthImage (raw depth, centre snapped to a full resolution texel) with u_ViewInfo = zFar/zNear, zFar, tan(fov/2) x and y (zNear from viewParms.zNear); the normal from the neighbour on each axis with the smaller depth step. Samples on a 7-turn spiral inside a disc of r_ssaoRadius world units (at most 12 % of the screen), each adds f³ · max((v·n − bias) / (v·v + ε), 0), f = r² − v·v; A = 1 − sum · strength · 5/N · r / r⁶. Per pixel rotation from interleaved gradient noise. Fades to 1 between 60 % and 100 % of r_ssaoDistance; sky (depth 1) and pixels whose disc is under two texels stay 1.
BlurdepthBlurShader[0] / [1]Horizontal into tr.quarterFbo[1], vertical into tr.screenSsaoFbo, depth aware (unchanged).
ApplyRB_PostProcessMultiplied over the HDR scene before tone mapping. Main view only (viewport ≥ half the screen, no water/car reflection views, no RDF_NOWORLDMODEL), so small extra views never get a stale AO image.

Normal & Specular Maps — Implementation

The OpenGL2 lightall shader already uses a normal map (light direction per vertex from the light grid, or the deluxe map) and a specular map (rgb = reflectance, alpha = gloss), and CollapseStagesToLightall looks for <texture>_nh, _n and _s next to the diffuse texture. Q3Rally ships almost none, so they are made at load time. Cvars: r_genNormalMaps (archive, latched, 1), r_genNormalStrength (archive, latched, 1).

PartWhereWhat it does
GeneratorR_GenerateSurfaceMaps (tr_image.c)Loads the diffuse file again (R_LoadImage, uncompressed sources only). Height = squared luminance minus a box blur of 1/24 of the size (painted light and shadow are not relief). Slopes from a 1 texel blur (central differences) plus a 1/128 size blur (±3 texels), scaled by size / 256 so the relief does not depend on the resolution; same signs as the old generator. Tiling textures wrap, clamped ones clamp. Writes <name>_n (IMGTYPE_NORMAL) and <name>_s (white, gloss 0.35–1 in alpha from the fine height), both with IMGFLAG_GENERATED, mip mapped like the diffuse texture. The work buffers (5 floats + 8 bytes per texel) come from malloc, not ri.Malloc: the 48 MB zone ran out on a 1254×1254 texture (q3r_sundown); the reloaded source is freed after the luminance pass.
WhenCollapseStagesToLightall (tr_shader.c)Only when no _nh / _n file exists, the stage has a lightmap (shader.lightmapIndex ≥ 0), no alpha test and the shader no polygon offset. The generated images are found by name for every further shader with the same texture. The old IMGFLAG_GENNORMALMAP path in R_FindImageFile is no longer triggered.

Bot AI

Racing Bot Waypoint System

Current state, identified issues, and the planned hybrid Ghost-Converter approach for improving bot racing quality.

Current State

What Works TodayServer loads .ghost files robustly (header/frames, size limit, fallbacks). Fastest matching ghost is selected as the bot route. Bots actively use waypoints for steering direction and approximate target speed. Legacy .bpd bezier paths from map entities still load at map start.

Identified Issues

1. Track Crossing Ambiguity Open
Per tick the nearest waypoint is searched geometrically. At track overlaps / crossings this can snap to the wrong section of the circuit.

Proposal: Cache a progress index per bot (forward-only with a small search window). Optionally blend checkpoint-progress + ghost-progress.
2. Fixed-Index Lookahead Open
bestIndex + 12 is track- and sampling-dependent. Dense or sparse samples cause too-short or too-long lookahead.

Proposal: Lookahead by time offset (+400–900 ms) or distance (+20–50 m) instead of fixed count. Or adaptive scaling based on current speed.
3. No Segment Projection Open
Steering targets a future waypoint but does not project onto the current segment (i→i+1). Causes unnecessary oscillation.

Proposal: Project position onto segment, derive tangential direction. Optionally apply Catmull-Rom smoothing.
4. Target Speed from Single Segment Open
Target speed estimated only from bestIndex and bestIndex+1 — susceptible to timing jitter in ghost frames.

Proposal: Average speed over a window (5–15 segments). Use curvature lookahead to decelerate early for corners.
5. No Route Quality Metric Open
Any parseable route is used even if noisy.

Proposal: Plausibility checks on load: monotone timestamps, min/max distances, outlier filter. Optional resampling to constant time grid.

Hybrid Ghost-Converter Plan

Status: Planned — Not Yet ImplementedThe ghost converter, in-game recording commands, and flexible corridor navigation are design documents. They are not present in the current production build.

Preferred Workflow

  1. Input: ghost/<map>/<run>.ghost
  2. Tool: ghost2bpd input.ghost output.bpd
  3. Output: maps/<map>.bpd

On conversion: ghost samples are resampled and smoothed, projected onto checkpoint segments, Bézier handles estimated from tangents and curvature.

Fallback: In-Game Record & Bake

  1. recordRacingLine start
  2. Drive a reference lap
  3. recordRacingLine stop
  4. recordRacingLine save

Saves as maps/<map>.bpd in the existing parser-compatible format.

Minimal-Invasive Implementation Strategy

  1. Introduce ghostRouteIndexHint per bot
  2. Nearest search only in local window around hint (e.g. [−8, +24])
  3. Lookahead by time offset instead of fixed waypoint count
  4. Speed smoothing over multiple segments

This stays fully compatible with the existing .ghost format while gaining robustness and pace consistency.

Expected Impact (Prioritised)

1Progress index + search windowBiggest stability improvement immediately
2Time/distance lookaheadBetter line choice across varying track layouts
3Segment projection + smoothed target speedLess steering jitter, more natural bot movement

Bug Investigations & Fixes

Internal post-mortems and fix documentation for non-trivial bugs.

Team Mode Entity Overflow

G_Spawn: no free entities in team game types Fixed

Symptom: Server aborts with G_Spawn: no free entities during any team-based game type. Entity dump shows hundreds of entries tagged noclass.

Root cause: CopyToBodyQue clones the extra collision bounds (frontBounds/rearBounds) from the dying vehicle so the corpse keeps its full collision hull. Body queue slots are recycled in a ring buffer — helper entities from a previous corpse may still exist when the slot is reused. The old code overwrote body->frontBounds/rearBounds pointers with new G_Spawn() results, leaving the old helpers orphaned. The leak quickly exhausted the entity pool in small-team modes with frequent deaths.

Fix: Each body-queue entry now owns two persistent helper entities created alongside the queue in InitBodyQue. When a corpse is copied into the slot the helpers are simply updated and relinked; when the corpse sinks they are unlinked instead of freed. A shared G_ResetExtraBBox helper keeps all code paths (body sinking, gibbing, etc.) consistent. Entity count stays flat regardless of death frequency.

Ghost Waypoint System Review

Waypoint ambiguity at track crossings Open
See the Bot Waypoints tab for the full analysis and proposed fixes. Root issue: nearest-waypoint search without a progress hint can snap to the wrong track section at overlaps.

v0.7d - Combat Bot Fixes

Combat bots not firing or resupplying reliably Fixed in v0.7d

Symptom: Bots visibly chased enemies and carried front weapons, but often failed to fire. On large combat maps they also kept fighting with weak or empty weapons instead of searching for better weapons or ammunition.

Root cause: Combat movement kept bots locked into circular chase patterns and the supply logic only looked for nearby goals. If no useful item was close enough, the bot immediately re-entered combat instead of selecting a longer-distance weapon or ammo goal.

Fix: Combat bots now use a phase-based attack movement that can break off, turn around, and re-engage. When supplies are needed, bots first check nearby goals and then fall back to a proper LTG_GETITEM weapon/ammo goal. Objective carriers keep their flag/cube duties. Elimination remains excluded because it is handled as a race/survival mode, not a regular combat mode.

False Final Lap warning after first frag Fixed in v0.7d

Symptom: finallap.ogg could play immediately after the first frag before the player's elimination position was known.

Root cause: CG_CheckEliminationWarning fell back to myPos = playersRemaining when the local player position had not been initialized yet.

Fix: The warning now exits early while the player position is unknown, preventing the false Final Lap audio trigger.

v0.7c — Gametype Enum Shift

DM dedicated server cycling maps immediately Fixed in v0.7c

Symptom: A dedicated server in DM mode immediately cycles through the map rotation without waiting for players.

Root cause: GT_SPRINT was inserted into the gametype_t enum in bg_public.h without explicit integer values, shifting GT_DEATHMATCH from 6 to 7 and all team gametypes by one. A server started with g_gametype 6 (DM) was therefore running GT_ELIMINATION (a race mode), which exits immediately when no players are present.

Fix: Assigned explicit integer values to all entries in gametype_t. Non-team gametypes retain v0.6c values (0–6), GT_SPRINT = 7. Team gametypes start at 16, leaving slots 8–15 for future non-team modes. See the Server Setup tab in the manual for the migration table.

Transport & Retry

  • Async POST via libcurl (same CURLM pattern as the ladder reporter).
  • HTTP 5xx or network failure → spool to telemetry/outbox, exponential backoff (5 s start, 5 min cap).
  • HTTP 202 queued → optional polling via GET /v1/matches/{matchId} (disabled by default).
  • Spool limited to sv_telemetryMaxBatch entries under fs_homepath.
